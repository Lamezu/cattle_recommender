# 📖 User Guide - Cattle Recommender

Welcome to the cattle recommendation system. This guide will help you navigate the application and understand how to get the best recommendations for your farm.

## 1. Login
To access, use your farmer identifier (ID). The system will automatically load your preferences and purchase history.

## 2. Main Dashboard
Upon entry, you will see four key sections:
*   **Recommended:** Cows suggested specifically for you based on what other similar farmers have purchased.
*   **Similar:** If you have been viewing a specific type of cow, you will see others with similar characteristics here.
*   **Most Purchased/Viewed:** Current market trends.

## 3. Interacting with Cattle
By clicking on a cow, you can:
*   **View Details:** Age, breed, and price.
*   **Rate:** Rate with stars so the system learns your tastes.
*   **Buy:** Record your purchase to improve future recommendations.

---
*Note: The system updates in real-time every time you interact with a cow.*
